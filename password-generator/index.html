Password Security Tips

  • Avoid using personal information like your birth year or name.
  • Steer clear of common patterns like "qwerty" or "123456".
  • Don't use more than two repeating consecutive characters (e.g., "111").
  • Combine uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • The longer the password, the more secure it is.
